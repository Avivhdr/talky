Talky

Hold fn, talk, let go. The text appears wherever your cursor is, with the "um"s taken out.

Download for macOS

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Drag Talky into Applications. It updates itself.

How it works

Hold fnRecords while you hold it, pastes when you let go.
Double-tap fnHands-free. Tap once more to stop.
Select text, tap right ⌥Rewrites the selection: formal, bullets, or an instruction you say out loud.
EscCancels.

What you need

A Groq API key for transcription. The free tier covers normal dictation.

An Anthropic API key if you want cleanup and rewrites. Without one, Talky pastes exactly what you said.

Keys stay on your Mac in ~/.config/talky/.env. Talky has no account and no telemetry. Audio goes to Groq, text goes to Anthropic, and nothing else leaves your machine.

Uninstall

Settings › Uninstall Talky. It turns off launch at login, clears its permissions, deletes its files and moves itself to the Trash.
