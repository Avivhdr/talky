One key. Any app.

Talky lives in the menu bar. It watches only its own keys, so everything else still reaches the app you are typing in.

Hold fn
Records while you hold it. When you let go, it transcribes, cleans up and pastes.
Double-tap fn
Hands-free. Tap fn once more, or click stop on the pill, to finish. It stops by itself after five minutes.
1–9 while recording

What you need

A Mac
macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Talky updates itself.
574 MB of space
For the Whisper speech model. Talky downloads it once, during setup.
An Anthropic API key
Optional. It turns on pipes, for what you say and for text you select. Without it, Talky types exactly what you said.

To remove it: Settings › Uninstall Talky. It turns off launch at login, clears its permissions, deletes its files and moves itself to the Trash.
